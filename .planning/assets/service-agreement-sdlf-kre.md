## 1. Scope of Services

### 1.1 Services Provided by SDLF Worldwide Solutions LLC

The Service Provider shall deliver the following services for the operation and growth of the Client's real estate business under the brand wirkaufendeineimmobilie.de:

**a) Platform Development and Technical Operations**
- Development, maintenance and hosting of the website wirkaufendeineimmobilie.de
- Database administration and technical infrastructure management
- Implementation of new features and technical improvements
- Server administration and security updates

**b) Digital Marketing Strategy and Execution**
- Development and execution of marketing strategies for lead generation
- Search engine optimization (SEO) for relevant keywords
- Creation and management of landing pages and conversion funnels

**c) Lead Generation and Funnel Management**
- Design and implementation of lead magnets (quizzes, guides, tools)
- Setup and management of email automation workflows
- Lead qualification and segmentation systems

**d) Content Production and Brand Management**
- Development of brand identity and visual design
- Creation of marketing copy, landing page content and sales materials
- Production of visual assets (graphics, diagrams, presentations)

**e) Data Analytics, Reporting and Business Intelligence**
- Tracking setup and conversion analytics
- Regular performance reporting
- Data-driven optimization recommendations

**f) Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Communication Systems**
- Setup and management of CRM tools (Brevo)
- Email template design and automation
- Customer communication workflow design

### 1.2 Services Provided by Kleinke Real Estate

The Client is responsible for all activities requiring the §34c trade license (Gewerbeerlaubnis) and direct customer interaction:

**a) Regulatory Compliance**
- Maintenance of the §34c Gewerbeordnung trade license for real estate brokerage
- Compliance with all applicable regulations (Makler- und Bauträgerverordnung, Geldwäschegesetz)
- Identification and verification of customers as required by the Geldwäschegesetz (GwG)

**b) Customer Interaction**
- Personal contact with sellers, buyers and investors
- Property inspections and valuations
- Negotiation of terms and conditions

**c) Transaction Processing**
- Coordination of notary appointments
- Contract management and completion of real estate transactions
- Commission invoicing and collection

**d) Local Market Expertise**
- Market knowledge for Leipzig and surrounding areas
- Property evaluation and pricing
- Network of local contacts (notaries, craftsmen, banks)

## 4. Term and Termination

### 4.1 Term

This agreement is entered into for an indefinite period, effective from the date specified above.

### 4.2 Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 3 months written notice to the end of a calendar month.

### 4.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate immediately for cause, including but not limited to:
- Material breach of this agreement that is not cured within 14 days after written notice
- Insolvency or bankruptcy of the other party
- Loss of required licenses (in particular the §34c Gewerbeerlaubnis)

### 4.4 Obligations Upon Termination

Upon termination:
- The Service Provider transfers all client data, access credentials and documentation to the Client
- The Service Provider deletes all personal data as specified in the AVV (Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag)
- Outstanding compensation is settled within 30 days
- The Client receives access to the codebase and hosting infrastructure

## 5. Arm's-Length Terms

The compensation agreed in Section 2 reflects market rates for comparable digital services in the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). Reference benchmarks:

- Freelance developer/digital strategist day rates in the DACH market (2025/2026)
- Agency fees for comparable scope (web development, marketing, lead generation)
- Revenue share models common in real estate marketing partnerships

Both parties confirm that the terms of this agreement are negotiated at arm's length and reflect fair market value for the services provided. This is documented to ensure compliance with transfer pricing rules applicable to related-party transactions involving a US entity.
